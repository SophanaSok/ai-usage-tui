# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. This applies in
the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone is acting on
the project's behalf.

## Reporting

Email **sokdevelopment@gmail.com** with `ai-usage-tui conduct` in the subject. Say what happened
and where, with links if there are any. You do not have to be the person it happened to.

A report is read by the maintainer and by nobody else, and is not discussed in public or with
the person it concerns beyond what acting on it requires. Expect an acknowledgement within 7
days.

This project has one maintainer. If the report is about the maintainer, or you would rather it
did not go to them, use GitHub's own channel instead: the `...` menu on any comment, or
<https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse>. GitHub acts on those without involving the
repository's owner.

## What happens next

The maintainer decides, and says what was decided to the person who reported. In rising order,
and skipping steps when the behaviour warrants it:

1. A private note to the person concerned, naming what was wrong.
2. Removing or editing the comment, or closing or locking the thread.
3. A temporary block from the repository.
4. A permanent block, and a report to GitHub.

Reports made in bad faith are themselves a breach of this code.
